# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Azhura welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not share personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, doxxing and sustained disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Reports should be sent privately through GitHub's repository reporting channels.
